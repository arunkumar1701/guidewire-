## PPT Slide Detailed Content

### **Slide 1: Title & Mission**
- **Title:** GigShield AI
- **Subtitle:** Parametric Income Protection for the Gig Economy
- **Vision:** "Transforming reactive insurance into real-time, AI-driven income protection."

### **Slide 2: The Income Gap (Problem Statement)**
- **The Challenge:** Per-task income models have no safety net.
- **Disruption Impact:** 20-30% drop in hours during weather events.
- **IMD Standards:** Aligning with India Meteorological Department disruption categories.

### **Slide 3: AI & Implementation Stack**
- **Risk Prediction:** XGBoost Ensemble Scoring.
- **Loss Estimation:** Gradient Boosting Regression.
- **Tech Stack:** Next.js (Web), Node.js (API), FastAPI (ML), PostgreSQL (DB).

### **Slide 4: Adversarial Defense & Cybersecurity**
- **Fraud Detection:** Graph-Based Ring Detection & Multi-Signal Validation.
- **Cybersecurity Architecture:**
    - **Data Integrity:** SHA-256 Hashing for sensor logs.
    - **Secure Communication:** TLS 1.3 Encryption.
    - **Identity & Access:** JWT-based IAM with MFA.
    - **Data Protection:** AES-256-GCM for PII.

### **Slide 5: Impact & Future Roadmap**
- **Value Proposition:** Reduces Basis Risk, Network-Level Defense, Zero Friction.
- **Roadmap:** Phase 1: Prototype Logic; Phase 2: Real-time API Integration; Phase 3: National Scale-up.
