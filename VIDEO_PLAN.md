# 2-Minute Demo Video Plan: GigShield AI

## Goal
To demonstrate a seamless, AI-powered parametric insurance experience for gig workers, focusing on the "Know Your Delivery Worker" theme.

## Timeline & Script

| Time | Scene | Visual | Audio/Narrative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00-0:15** | **The Problem** | Montage: Zepto worker in heavy rain, flooded roads, empty delivery bags. | "Meet Rohan. A delivery partner whose income stops when the rain starts. For him, a flood isn't just a weather event; it's a financial crisis." |
| **0:15-0:30** | **The Solution** | UI: GigShield Landing Page. Clean, mobile-first design. | "Introducing GigShield. The first AI-powered parametric insurance platform built specifically for the quick commerce workforce." |
| **0:30-1:00** | **Workflow: Onboarding & Policy** | UI: Rohan signs up. Shows AI risk scoring and dynamic weekly premium (₹49/week). | "Rohan joins in seconds. Our AI Risk Engine analyzes his delivery zone and historical disruption data to offer a personalized weekly premium that fits his payout cycle." |
| **1:00-1:30** | **The Simulation: Rainstorm Trigger** | UI: Admin dashboard 'Trigger Rainstorm'. Map turns red. | "When our Parametric Trigger Engine detects 10mm/hr rainfall via OpenWeather APIs, it automatically triggers claims. No paperwork. No waiting." |
| **1:30-1:50** | **Validation & Payout** | UI: Fraud detection check pass. Payout notification (UPI). | "Our Fraud Detection model verifies GPS data instantly. Rohan receives an automated payout to cover his lost hours—keeping his family secure while the roads are closed." |
| **1:50-2:00** | **Conclusion** | Logo: GigShield. Slogan: 'Insuring the Invisible Workforce'. | "GigShield: Parametric protection for the gig economy. Phase-1: Ideated. Ready for Impact." |

## Demo Simulation Strategy
*   **Trigger:** We will use the `mock_data_generator.js` to simulate a "Heavy Rain" event.
*   **Visualization:** The Admin Dashboard will show real-time logs of the API data crossing the threshold and the subsequent automated claim generation.
*   **Persona focus:** Always refer to Rohan and his specific quick-commerce constraints (weekly payouts, zone-based work).
